Your transportation cheat sheet

No MetroCard needed. Each person taps their own iPhone (Apple Pay), contactless card, or OMNY card at the subway turnstile. Standard subway fare: $3 each. Same device/card for the 7-day $35 fare cap. NYC Ferry to DUMBO is a separate $5 ticket and does not use OMNY.

👣 How to use the subway

1. Find the station entrance at the cross streets below. 2. Check the signs for the correct train and Uptown/Downtown direction. 3. Tap once at the turnstile with your iPhone/card. 4. Follow the platform signs. 5. Exit at your stop — no tap-out required.

The subway operates frequently, but service changes and entrance closures are possible. Use the live MTA app or Google Maps on the day if a train is rerouted.

Step-by-step rides

Airport transport

🟨 Recommended — official yellow taxi

At LGA, follow signs to the official taxi stand. Tell the driver: Hotel Edison, 228 West 47th Street. Expect a metered fare, applicable surcharges/tolls and a tip. Allow roughly 30–60 minutes on arrival, longer if traffic is heavy. For the return, leave the hotel around 7:15 AM Saturday.

📱 Uber / Lyft alternative

Check the actual pickup location and price in the app after landing. Compare the all-in price to the yellow cab. Do not accept unsolicited rides in the terminal.

🚍 Cheapest alternative — Q70 + E train

From LGA take the free Q70 LaGuardia Link to Jackson Heights–Roosevelt Av / 74 St–Broadway. Tap OMNY for the Manhattan-bound E train, exit at 50 St (8th Avenue), and walk south/east to Hotel Edison on W 47th. Approximately 55–80 minutes with luggage; check service on the day.
